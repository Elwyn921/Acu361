# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-22)

First public release.

- `data/points.json`, `data/points.csv`: 361 WHO 2008 point codes, 670 points (309 bilateral codes x 2 + 52 midline codes) on the Z-Anatomy male standing body, with the nearest bone and the distance to it.
- `data/meridians.json`: surface lines of the 14 meridians on the skin (66 segments).
- `data/rulers.json`: 27 proportional bone (skeletal) measurements of WHO 2008 with 31 instances on the body and 19 derived landmarks.
- `data/body/*.glb`: seven anatomical layers of the Z-Anatomy body (2,812 objects).
- `data/manifest.json`, `schema/`, `scripts/validate.py`.
